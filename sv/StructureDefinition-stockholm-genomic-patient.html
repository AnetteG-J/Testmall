Resursprofil: Stockholm Genomic Patient

Officiell URL: https://pub.regionstockholm.se/fhir/ImplementationGuide/Testmall/StructureDefinition/stockholm-genomic-patient Version: 2.3.0
Draft per 2026-10-29 Beräkningsbart namn: StockholmGenomicPatient

Minimal Patient profile for genomic studies. Exposes only the key elements required: identifier, gender, birthDate and deceased.
